Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Who uses this app

TV Terminal is a personal, single-user app. Its only user is its owner, on their own TV.

What data it reads

With the owner's permission, it reads the owner's YouTube data (liked songs and playlists) in read-only mode, only to show them on the owner's TV home screen.

Where data is kept

The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own server. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or analytics.

How to remove access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The stored token is then deleted from the owner's server.
